RDX turns folders and `.md` or `.mdx` files into a documentation site. Start by editing
this page or adding new pages inside `docs/`.

## Start here

- [Write your first page](./writing-pages.mdx) and organize the sidebar.
- [Configure your site](./configure.mdx) with your name, logo, and navigation.
- [Try Markdown](../02-examples/markdown.md), then explore [Callouts](../02-examples/callouts.mdx),
  [Tabs](../02-examples/tabs.mdx), [Accordion](../02-examples/accordion.mdx), and
  [Steps](../02-examples/steps.mdx).
- [Share a page as Markdown](../02-examples/markdown-exports.mdx) or open the
  documentation index at `/docs/llms.txt`.
- Explore [code blocks](../02-examples/code-blocks.mdx),
  [diagrams](../02-examples/diagrams.mdx), and
  [keyboard shortcuts](../02-examples/keyboard-shortcuts.mdx).
