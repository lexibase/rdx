title: Search works in the language you’re reading
---

If you switched your docs to Portuguese or another language and opened search,
RDX could load the index but still tell you search was unavailable. Search now
shows results from the language you’re reading, so you can find a page and stay
in that language when you open it.

This also works when your docs live under a subpath. For example, a result on
`/my-tool/pt-br/docs` opens under `/my-tool/pt-br/docs/...`.

If your site uses the i18n plugin, update to RDX 6.3.1 to get the fix.
