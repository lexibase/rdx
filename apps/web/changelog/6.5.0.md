title: Help readers find their next step
---

When someone lands on a guide, they can see what to read first, where to go
next, and when the page last changed. When you move a guide, you can keep its
old URL working for people who bookmarked or shared it.

## Give readers a starting point and a next step

Add `prerequisites` to show a **Before you begin** row below the description.
The [Build and deployment](/docs/6.5.0/deployment) guide uses it to point readers
to installation and configuration before the first build command.

Use `related` to suggest what to read after the guide. See the links at the
bottom of [Writing pages](/docs/6.5.0/create-docs). Both fields take relative
Markdown paths, keep readers in their current edition and language, and are
validated by `rdx check`.

## See when a page changed

**Last updated** appears above the previous and next page buttons when RDX
can determine the date from Git. Published editions keep the date recorded
when their snapshot was created.

## Keep shared links working when you move a page

Add `redirectFrom` to the moved page's frontmatter and list its old `/docs/`
paths. RDX redirects those paths to the new page in the same edition and
language. `rdx check` reports conflicts before deployment. See
[Keep links to a moved page working](/docs/6.5.0/create-docs#keep-links-to-a-moved-page-working)
for an example.
